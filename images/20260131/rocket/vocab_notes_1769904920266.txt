The Last Spike: Canadian Nationhood and the Contribution of Chinese Laborers

1. Transcontinental [/ˌtrænzˌkɑntəˈnɛntḷ/] 橫貫大陸的；跨越洲際的
The completion of the transcontinental railway was vital for the political unity of the young Canadian nation.
橫貫大陸鐵路的完工對於這個年輕加拿大國家的政治統一至關重要。

2. Monumental [/ˌmɑnjəˈmɛntḷ/] 巨大的；具有深遠意義的
Building a railway through the Rocky Mountains was a monumental task that changed the course of Canadian history.
在洛磯山脈修建鐵路是一項巨大的任務，它改變了加拿大的歷史進程。

3. Indispensable [/ˌɪndɪˈspɛnsəbḷ/] 不可或缺的；絕對必要的
The contribution of thousands of Chinese laborers was indispensable to the successful completion of the railway.
成千上萬華工的貢獻對於鐵路的成功完工是不可或缺的。

4. Perilous [/ˈpɛrələs/] 險惡的；非常危險的
Laborers faced perilous conditions, including handling explosives and working on steep mountain cliffs.
工人們面臨著險惡的環境，包括處理炸藥以及在陡峭的山崖上工作。

5. Culmination [/ˌkʌlməˈneʃən/] 頂點；終點；（長期努力後的）結果
The driving of 'The Last Spike' was the culmination of years of grueling physical labor and political negotiation.
「最後一根釘」的敲入是多年艱苦體力勞動和政治談判的最終結果。

6. Sovereignty [/ˈsɑvrənti/] 主權；統治權
The railway helped Canada assert its sovereignty over the western territories and prevent American expansion.
這條鐵路幫助加拿大維護了其對西部領土的主權，並防止了美國的擴張。

7. Exploitation [/ˌɛksplɔɪˈteʃən/] 剝削；開發利用
History books now acknowledge the exploitation and systemic discrimination faced by Chinese workers during the construction.
歷史書現在承認了華工在建設期間所面臨的剝削和系統性歧視。

8. Unification [/ˌjunəfəˈkeʃən/] 統一；聯合
The unification of the East and West coasts fulfilled a promise made during the formation of the Confederation.
東西海岸的統一履行了聯邦成立期間所做出的承諾。

9. Commemorate [/kəˈmɛməˌret/] 紀念；慶祝
Various monuments now exist to commemorate the sacrifices made by those who built the Canadian Pacific Railway.
現在有多座紀念碑用以紀念那些建造加拿大太平洋鐵路的人們所做出的犧牲。
